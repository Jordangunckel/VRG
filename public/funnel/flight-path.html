Why this matters

Every job needs somewhere to go.

Here's what we've learned after years on the roof and in the back office: every job needs a next move. The moment a job has nowhere to go, it stops moving — and a job that stops moving is a job that's quietly dying. No follow-up. No next call. Nobody watching it. We've seen it more times than we can count: the deal didn't fall through, it fell through the cracks.

The Flight Path fixes that with one golden rule: every status gets an entry and an exit automation. The entry decides what happens the moment a job lands there. The exit decides what triggers it to move to the next status. Nothing waits on someone to remember it — so no job ever gets forgotten.
