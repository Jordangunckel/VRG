Why this matters

Every job needs somewhere to go.

Best practice is simple: every job always has a next status to move to. The second a job goes stagnant, it falls through the cracks — no follow-up, no next step, nobody watching it. We've seen it cost roofers real money, over and over. The Flight Path fixes that with one golden rule: every status gets an entry and an exit automation. The entry decides what happens the moment a job lands there; the exit decides what triggers it to move to the next status. So no job ever gets forgotten.
